Eligibility Criteria for IDFC FIRST Select Credit Card

  • Minimum Age:

    21 years for salaried and 23 years for self-employed applicants.

  • Monthly Income:

    Minimum ₹30,000 (salaried) or suitable turnover for self-employed applicants.

  • Credit Score:

    Good credit history recommended (CIBIL 700+ improves approval chances).

  • Resident Status:

    Indian residents with valid KYC, PAN and supporting documents.

After eligibility and document verification, you can apply online and may receive a digital/virtual card instantly (subject to bank process). Always confirm latest T&C, fees, and offers on the official IDFC FIRST website.
