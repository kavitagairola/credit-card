Eligibility Criteria for HDFC Bank Pixel Play Credit Card

  • Minimum Age:

    21 years for salaried and 23 years for self-employed applicants.

  • Monthly Income:

    Minimum ₹25,000 (salaried) or ₹3 lakh annual ITR (self-employed).

  • Credit Score:

    Good credit history (CIBIL score 700+) preferred for approval.

  • Resident Status:

    Indian residents with valid KYC and PAN details.

After eligibility and verification, you can instantly get the digital version of your HDFC Pixel Play Credit Card via the HDFC Bank app. Activate your card, customize your rewards, and start playing to earn cashback from day one.
