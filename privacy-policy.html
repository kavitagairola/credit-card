Information We Collect

To provide you with personalized credit card recommendations and facilitate applications, we collect the following information:

Personal Information:

  • Identity Information: Full name as per Aadhaar/PAN card, date of birth, gender
  • Contact Details: Mobile number, email address, residential address, PIN code
  • KYC Documents: PAN card number, Aadhaar number, passport/voter ID (if applicable)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Credit Card Recommendations: Analyzing your profile to suggest the most suitable credit cards based on your income, spending patterns, and preferences
  • Application Processing: Submitting your credit card application to our partner banks (HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI, IDFC First, RBL, etc.) on your behalf
  • Application Status Updates: Keeping you informed about your application progress, approval status, and card delivery through SMS, email, and WhatsApp
  • Customer Support: Responding to your queries, providing assistance with applications, and resolving issues
  • Personalization: Customizing your experience on our platform with relevant card offers and promotions
  • Marketing Communications: Sending you promotional offers, new card launches, and special deals (you can opt-out anytime)
  • Platform Improvement: Analyzing user behavior to enhance website functionality and user experience
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting and preventing fraudulent applications and suspicious activities
  • Compliance: Meeting regulatory requirements and cooperating with law enforcement when legally required

Sharing Information with Partner Banks

When you apply for a credit card through Dfinkard Hub, we share your information with the relevant bank or financial institution. This includes:

  • Partner Banks: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, State Bank of India (SBI), IDFC First Bank, RBL Bank, Yes Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, IndusInd Bank, and other RBI-approved banks
  • Information Shared: Personal details, contact information, financial data, KYC documents, and employment information required for credit assessment
  • Purpose: To enable banks to process your credit card application, verify your information, conduct credit checks, and make approval decisions
  • Bank's Privacy Policy: Each partner bank has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data. We encourage you to review their policies

Important: Once your information is shared with a bank, their privacy practices and data security measures will apply. Dfinkard Hub is not responsible for how partner banks use or protect your information beyond the application process.

Personal Data Protection & Security

Security facilities have been introduced by us for protecting your personal data when you enter, transmit or work with your personal information. We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using 256-bit SSL/TLS
  • Secure Data Storage: Personal information is stored on encrypted servers with restricted access
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel can access your information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • OTP Verification: Mobile number verification through One-Time Passwords for application submission
  • Data Minimization: We collect only the information necessary for credit card applications
  • Secure Document Upload: KYC documents are transmitted and stored securely

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your information for different periods depending on its purpose:

  • Legal Requirements: As mandated by applicable laws and regulations

After the retention period, your information is securely deleted or anonymized.
